I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Central Florida (UCF), working with Dr. Mohamed Abdel-Aty at SST Lab. Before I joined, I received my Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from KAIST, South Korea, under the supervision of Dr. Hwasoo Yeo at AIxMobility Lab.

My research focuses on human driving behavior and its effects on traffic flow and safety, combining traffic flow modeling and empirical analysis. During my Ph.D., I developed microscopic and mesoscopic traffic simulation models that reflect human driver characteristics and integrated them into a multi-level simulation for large-scale urban networks. I also developed a real-time traffic prediction system that combines simulation with artificial intelligence (AI) models. At UCF, I have extended this focus to traffic safety by conducting driving simulator experiments and analyzing connected-vehicle data to derive practical safety implications.

Research

Traffic Flow Theory and Traffic Simulation

  • Developing a micro-meso hybrid traffic simulation that assigns simulation resolution dynamically across large urban networks, and extending it to the macro scale with multi-class traffic.
  • Utilizing traffic simulation for ITS by generating synthetic traffic scenarios and combining them with data-driven methods to improve prediction performance under incident cases.

Human Driving Behavior

  • Modeling driver behavior from high-resolution vehicle trajectory and naturalistic driving data in highway and urban areas.
  • Running driving simulator experiments to measure how speed choice, attention, and risk-taking shift with road and traffic conditions.

Traffic Safety

  • Developing crash risk and crash frequency models across highway and urban areas using detector, road network, and vehicle trajectory data.
